THE CHALLENGE

Why do most companies fail to capitalize on their data?

1

Full dependency on a technical team

Every simple analytical question requires a data engineer to write a SQL query, slowing decisions and overloading the IT team.

2

Scattered, unreliable data

Spreadsheets and disconnected databases with no statistical profiling or quality checks, so decisions rely on unverified numbers.

3

Reports built manually and take days

Turning raw numbers into an executive-ready report consumes analysts' time instead of focusing on strategic decisions.
